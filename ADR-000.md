# ADR-000: Atlas Engineering Principles

## Principle 1 — Integrate, Don't Replace
Atlas integrates with existing infrastructure instead of replacing databases, cloud providers, Kubernetes, or application code.

---

## Principle 2 — Humans Stay in Control
AI assists engineers. Critical actions always require human approval.

---

## Principle 3 — Every Recommendation Needs Evidence
Atlas should explain why it reached a conclusion by referencing telemetry, logs, traces, historical incidents, or documentation.

---

## Principle 4 — Understand Before Acting
The goal is to understand incidents before suggesting remediation.

---

## Principle 5 — Observability First
Every component of Atlas should be observable through metrics, logs, and traces.

---

## Principle 6 — Security by Default
Security is built into every layer, not added later.

---

## Principle 7 — Performance is Measurable
Every optimization should be backed by benchmarks rather than assumptions.

---

## Principle 8 — Documentation is Code
Architectural decisions and implementation details should be documented alongside the code.

---

## Principle 9 — Build for Production
Every feature should be designed with reliability, scalability, and maintainability in mind.

---

## Principle 10 — Continuous Learning
Atlas should improve over time by learning from previous incidents and engineer feedback.
