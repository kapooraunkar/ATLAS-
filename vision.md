## Why Atlas Exists

Modern observability platforms are excellent at collecting and visualizing telemetry, but they still leave engineers responsible for understanding incidents, reasoning about root causes, predicting future failures, and deciding how to respond.

Atlas exists to bridge that gap.

Rather than replacing existing observability platforms, databases, cloud providers, or application code, Atlas integrates with them to provide an additional layer of infrastructure intelligence.

By combining observability data with AI, machine learning, knowledge graphs, historical incidents, and retrieval-augmented generation, Atlas helps engineering teams understand what happened, why it happened, what is likely to happen next, and what actions should be considered.

Atlas is designed to augment engineers not replace them.


## What Atlas Does

Atlas focuses on four core capabilities:

- Understand infrastructure through telemetry and contextual data.
- Reason about incidents using AI and historical knowledge.
- Predict future failures before they become production incidents.
- Assist engineers with explainable recommendations and remediation plans.

Every recommendation is backed by evidence, and engineers remain in control of critical decisions.


## What Atlas Is Not

Atlas is not:

- A replacement for Datadog, Grafana, Dynatrace, or Elastic.
- A replacement for databases or cloud infrastructure.
- A replacement for Kubernetes.
- A replacement for application developers.
- An autonomous system that modifies production code.

Atlas is an intelligence layer that works alongside existing observability platforms.
